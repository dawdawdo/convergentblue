Risking the integrity of the divergentblue site, here's a list of internet videos:

---

[Brother, Can You Spare Me a Job](http://www.brothercanyouspareajob.com/ "Brother, Can You Spare Me a Job") - Tom Neely and Greg Saunders
A political commentary on the "booming" American economy. 

---

[I Like Pandas](http://www.jessicaborutski.com/ "I Like Pandas") - Jessica Borutski
Very professional look and great techno-cartoony music by Plone. Even Panda-haters will enjoy the twist ending. 

---

[Pirate Baby Cabana Battle Streetfight 2006](http://www.selectparks.net/modules.php?file=article&name=News&sid=442 "Pirate Baby Cabana Battle Streetfight 2006") - Paul Robertson
If the name hasn't already magnetized your mouse cursor, skip ahead. This is a 16-bit console game-style animation drawing elements from what apprars to be many years of video gaming. Music by [Cornel Wilczek](http://www.quamusic.com/) . Note: you might want to blindfold the kids, the elderly, those who are or may become pregnant, and those with a weak stomach for this one.

---

 [Smash Brizzle](http://divergentblue.com/?item_id=1&cat_id=3&subcat_id=1) - Stephen Abrams
This remix of video taken from [Super Smash Brothers](http://divergentblue.com/?item_id=1&cat_id=3&subcat_id=1) Melee is not quite on par with the other toons here, but [I](/) never [miss](/) a [self-plugging](/) opportunity. Music: Da Pipes by the esteemed [Franz Keller](http://www.franzkeller.com/)
